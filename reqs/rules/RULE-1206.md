**Rule 12.6.** A requirement the front end can check must be checked at the front end, in the author's words, and not left to Tint or a driver.

- Rationale: WGSL checks each requirement at the earliest opportunity, and for TypeShade the earliest opportunity is the source line.
- Derives from: [Errors](https://gpuweb.github.io/gpuweb/wgsl/#errors) ("each requirement will be checked at the earliest opportunity"); surface §3 and §7.
- Enforced by: `TS8029`, `TS8031`, `TS8036`, `TS8038`, `TS8068`, and the rest of the front-end codes, among them `TS8002` for a type the file declares nowhere, which used to be emitted as a struct of that name for Tint to refuse (`src/compiler/ts/unknown-names.test.ts`); the requirements proposal 0008 moved forward from Tint: a `for` update the lowering does not count, refused instead of dropped with its loop (Rule 7.5), an operator WGSL has no overload for on its operands' kind (Rule 7.1), a vector of bools in a uniform or storage binding (Rule 6.8), a decorator nothing reads, `@group` and `@binding` on a declaration among them (Rule 6.7), and a barrier, a derivative or a `workgroupUniformLoad` after a non-uniform `break` or `continue` (Rule 8.5), each pinned where that rule's Enforced-by names it, and a loop's hidden counter, which an author's `_w` or `_i` reached (Rule 2.2), pinned by `src/compiler/ts/loop-shapes.test.ts`; the debts of Appendix B are the requirements not yet moved forward.
