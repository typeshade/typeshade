**Rule 4.5.** A TypeScript type the compiler cannot represent on a target must be refused in one sentence naming the reason and what to write instead; the list is surface §28 and is not repeated here.

- Rationale: the constraint is the target's, and the sentence has to say so at the line the author wrote. The test is what the compiler can represent (Rule 1.5), not whether WGSL has a word for the type.
- Derives from: `changes/0100-typescript-meaning.md` (section 2); surface §28 (roadmap 0.3 item T10); [Plain Types](https://gpuweb.github.io/gpuweb/wgsl/#plain-types-section) for what a value type is.
