**Rule 3.3.** A declaration named by a WGSL keyword or reserved word must receive a fresh WGSL spelling, with every reference rewritten, whatever its kind (Proposal 0070). The record names the spelling beside the authored name where the host reads it.

- Rationale: WGSL must contain no reserved identifier, while a legal TypeScript name can be represented without changing its value or authored identity.
- Derives from: WGSL's keywords and reserved words, carried as `WGSL_RESERVED` in `src/core/reserved-words.ts`.
- Enforced by: the spelling plan (`planSpellings` in `src/core/spelling-plan.ts`) and `spellModule` for module-scope names and entry parameters, `sanitizeVariableNames` through `wgslBackend` for locals and helper parameters, and `src/compiler/ts/reserved-names.test.ts` (surface §62).
