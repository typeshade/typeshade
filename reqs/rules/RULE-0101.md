**Rule 1.1.** A `"use typeshade"` program must mean what this document defines.
A form borrowed from ECMAScript means what ECMAScript gives it, and a form borrowed from WGSL means what WGSL gives it, unless a rule of this document gives it another meaning and records the difference and its reason.
The WGSL the compiler emits must be a program WGSL accepts, and must compute that meaning.

- Rationale: TypeShade is a language of its own that borrows how an author writes from TypeScript and what the surface can express from WGSL. The source's meaning is the default because an author reads a borrowed form by what it means there; a difference is allowed where it serves the GPU, and it is written down where the author can find it.
- Derives from: `changes/0100-typescript-meaning.md` (section 1, the principle); [WGSL Module](https://gpuweb.github.io/gpuweb/wgsl/#wgsl-module) and [Introduction](https://gpuweb.github.io/gpuweb/wgsl/#intro) for the emitted program.
- Enforced by: the compile gate (`scripts/compile-gate.ts`), which hands every registered example to Tint; the open units of `docs/typeshade-plan.md` are listed in Appendix B.
