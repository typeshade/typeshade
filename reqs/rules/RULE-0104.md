**Rule 1.4.** Where ECMAScript has a form that carries the GPU meaning, the surface must borrow TypeScript's spelling; a WGSL spelling is borrowed only where no ECMAScript spelling carries the meaning, and the borrowing must keep every WGSL capability reachable (Rule 11.14).
A form may be borrowed in part: the members, the positions or the meaning it takes are those the rules give it.

- Rationale: the WGSL vocabulary is there to say what TypeScript cannot say, such as a value's width or an address space. It is not a second spelling an author has to learn for what TypeScript already says.
- Derives from: `changes/0100-typescript-meaning.md` (section 1, the first two requirements); the roadmap's first rule, "ordinary TypeScript first".
- Enforced by: review (Rule 13.1); Appendix B, under Rule 1.1, for the spellings the tree still offers twice.
