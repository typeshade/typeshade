**Rule 7.3.** An empty `switch` case directly above a case with a body is one clause with several selectors (`case 0: case 1:` is WGSL's `case 0, 1:`); an empty case with no case below it or directly above `default:`, and an empty `default:` with a case after it, must be refused; a case body must not fall through to the next case.

- Rationale: WGSL's `switch` has no fall-through, but a clause may carry several selectors, which is what stacked empty cases mean in TypeScript; the three refused shapes have no clause the compiler can join them to (surface §52; #168 changed this rule, which refused every empty case).
- Derives from: [Switch Statement](https://gpuweb.github.io/gpuweb/wgsl/#switch-statement).
- Enforced by: `src/compiler/ts/switch-array.test.ts` (`shares one body between two selectors, on both targets`); `TS8017 SWITCH_CASE` for the three refused shapes (`switch case 1 has no body: …`, `switch case 1 sits above "default:" with no body of its own. …`, `"default:" has no body of its own and a clause follows it. …`); a non-empty case that ends without `break` is lowered as if it had one, which is a silent divergence from TypeScript (Appendix B).
