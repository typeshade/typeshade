**Rule 8.5.** A collective operation (a derivative, an implicit-LOD texture sample, a barrier) must be in uniform control flow.

- Rationale: a barrier outside uniform control flow is a shader-creation error.
  A derivative or an implicit-LOD sample outside it triggers the `derivative_uniformity` diagnostic, whose default severity is `error` and which a WGSL author can lower or turn off with a `diagnostic` filter.
  The compiler performs the analysis at the source line (Rule 12.6) and emits a filter only where the author writes `@diagnostic` on an entry (surface §54).
  A derivative or an implicit-LOD sample the compiler can recompute before the statement that made the flow non-uniform is rewritten so that it is in uniform control flow; the author does not move it (change 0132).
- Derives from: [Uniformity](https://gpuweb.github.io/gpuweb/wgsl/#uniformity); [Synchronization Built-in Functions](https://gpuweb.github.io/gpuweb/wgsl/#sync-builtin-functions) ("all synchronization functions must only be invoked in uniform control flow"); [Derivative Built-in Functions](https://gpuweb.github.io/gpuweb/wgsl/#derivative-builtin-functions) ("trigger a derivative_uniformity diagnostic if uniformity analysis cannot prove the call is in uniform control flow"); [Filterable Triggering Rules](https://gpuweb.github.io/gpuweb/wgsl/#filterable-triggering-rules) (default severity `error`).
- Enforced by: the derivative rewrite (`src/core/passes/derivative-rewrite.ts`, change 0132), which computes the gradients, the derivative or the call with no gradient form at the latest uniform statement before the call and samples with `textureSampleGrad` inside, run by `compileTsSource` before the report and pinned by `src/core/passes/derivative-rewrite.test.ts` in both halves; `TS8052 UNIFORMITY` for a derivative or an implicit-LOD sample the rewrite cannot reach, naming the reason, and for a barrier or a `workgroupUniformLoad`, reached under a condition on a non-uniform value (an `if`, a `switch`, a loop's condition, the left side of `&&` or `||`, the condition of a `?:` that WGSL writes as an `if`), or after a `return`, `break` or `continue` taken under one, naming the value as the author wrote it and the statement (#161, proposal 0008); `@diagnostic("off", "derivative_uniformity")` on the entry emits `diagnostic(off, derivative_uniformity);` and lifts the derivative half, never the barrier half; pinned by `src/core/passes/uniformity.test.ts` and `src/compiler/ts/barriers.test.ts`.
