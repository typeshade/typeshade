**Rule 3.4.** A local variable, parameter, module constant or module variable that collides with a GLSL ES 3.00 identifier restriction must be renamed consistently by the GLSL writer; helper function renaming retains its existing behavior. A struct, field, override or binding whose GLSL spelling is reserved receives a planned GLSL spelling (Proposal 0070), and its record carries `emittedGlsl` where that spelling differs from the WGSL one. No diagnostic is reported for it.

- Rationale: GLSL's variable spellings can change without changing source values. The remaining names belong to interface or host contracts; GLSL is the second target: its spelling leaves the WGSL name as authored, and a GLSL word no longer stops the GLSL emit.
- Derives from: ANGLE at shader version 300, including the `gl_` prefix and `__` shape restrictions, and the shared `GLSL_ES300_RESERVED` authority.
- Enforced by: the GLSL spellings of the spelling plan (`planSpellings` in `src/core/spelling-plan.ts`), `sanitizeVariableNames` and `sanitizeReservedIdents` in `src/core/backends/glsl-sanitize.ts`, and `src/compiler/ts/reserved-names.test.ts`.
